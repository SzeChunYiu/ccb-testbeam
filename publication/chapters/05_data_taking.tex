\section{Data taking and event selections}

\subsection{Run families}

Current configuration files define Sample-I and Sample-II analysis populations and separate calibration populations. These configuration roles are useful for reproducible processing but are not a substitute for the primary hardware run log. The run-role discrepancy discussed under issue \#962 means that calibration/analysis assignment must be bound to the exact fitted object before timing or response calibration can be described as held out.

The historical S00 selected-pulse product contains 640,737 B-stack pulses. That count is deterministic for the fixed historical producer/input set, but the canonical claim ledger keeps this claim GATED (promotion blocked) because the raw waveform width, raw-to-sorted lineage and channel-polarity/hardware contracts are not all independently closed. Selected-pulse counts are not detector efficiencies.

\subsection{Longitudinal topology in the existing selected products}

Existing source-bound selected-data studies show different B-stack occupancy patterns for the declared Sample-I and Sample-II populations: one population is more concentrated near the entrance while the other contains a larger downstream fraction. This is the central beam-data range-stack result. The pre-threshold 8-channel-by-16-sample event product has been regenerated and analysed (\#1318): 33 runs and 1{,}096{,}728 events (Sample~I 798{,}694; Sample~II 298{,}034) with frozen amplitude/polarity definitions and a run-block bootstrap (1{,}000 replicates, seed 1318). Under the measured polarity map v2 (\#954; the previous v1 convention is falsified for channels 2--7), the normalized amplitude profiles show Sample~I moderately concentrated at the entrance (B2 35.3\%, B8 25.2\%) while Sample~II extends further downstream (B2 15.3\%, B8 29.6\%): the B8/B2 amplitude-share ratio rises from 0.71 (Sample~I) to 1.94 (Sample~II). Each stave is read out by a duplicate channel pair, so the stave assignment is carried as a nuisance envelope: under the odd-channel hypothesis the ratio rises from 0.64 to 1.28, preserving the direction (\#1383). The earlier v1-based profile (87.4\%/72.7\% entrance concentration; $7.6\times$ deepest-stave ratio) is withdrawn as a polarity artifact. A threshold scan at 500/750/1000~ADC preserves the direction of the difference while compressing its magnitude (B8/B2 ratio contrast from $2.7\times$ at threshold 0 to $\approx 1.2\times$), so the quantitative profile remains GATED in the claim ledger (CL-1318-002; \texttt{allowed\_status\_validated=NO}) until a threshold/baseline contract closes.

\begin{evidencebox}
Until the final raw-event rerun and hardware run/trigger record close, the paper should call the existing difference a run-family-dependent longitudinal-topology indication. Interpreting it uniquely as a trigger effect would confound trigger changes with possible run-period beam/electronics changes, and calling the deepest active readout a stopping layer would additionally assume a range convention that the sparse readout does not itself establish.
\end{evidencebox}

\subsection{Monte Carlo population comparison}

The corresponding MC population is formed with \evidence{MC\_TRIGGER\_PROXY}. Under the corrected estimator contract (\#1046: unique truth-TrackID counting on the regenerated 2M-event campaign, event-level bootstrap $16$--$84\%$ intervals, provenance-bound to input sha256 \texttt{0d8c8275\ldots}), the coincidence-like population is deuteron-enriched in the first B layer by track flux ($59.3\%$, CI $57.7$--$60.7\%$) and deuteron-dominated by first-layer deposited-energy share ($85.5\%$), while the entering-any-layer population is proton-dominated by track flux ($73.4\%$, CI $73.0$--$73.7\%$; deuterons still carry $42.0\%$ of its first-layer deposited energy). Track-flux, event-presence and deposited-energy compositions differ by up to $26$ percentage points for the same population and are therefore reported as separate observables; a single species fraction without its estimator is not well defined. The A-arm composition is proton-led in both populations ($p\approx65\%$) and carries negligible discriminating power between them. The proxy can show that the transport model contains physically plausible stopping/penetrating populations, but it cannot authorise a hardware-trigger efficiency or a beam-data species mixture: the real trigger electronics remain external, and the simulation-side migration to an instrumented selection is quantified below (\evidence{MC\_TRIGGER\_MIGRATION}); the source-level cross-section uncertainty contract (\#1179) is now closed with a $\pm3\%$ source-model envelope, but the campaign itself was generated at central values, so campaign-level propagation remains bounded only at source level.

\subsection{Trigger-definition migration in the simulation}
\label{sec:trigger-migration}

The separation between the first-stack-layer proxy and a hardware-like selection has been quantified inside the simulation (\#1045). A geometry revision instruments the real baseline trigger counters as dedicated sensitive volumes (the \texttt{Trig\_stack\_1}/\texttt{Trig\_stack\_2} logical volumes split into T1/T2 trigger counters at $r=99$~cm; geometry receipt and sha256 digests recorded in ADR-1045), introducing no invented detector material. A $10^6$-event campaign on the authorising corrected-source chain reproduces the two-arm proxy population exactly ($554$ events), establishing that the revision is a geometry-only delta. Classifying every event jointly against both definitions at a per-hit deposit of $1$~MeV and a $15$~ns coincidence window gives, in the MC two-arm population, $165$ events passing both definitions, $389$ proxy-only and $195$ hardware-only: a two-arm retention of $0.298\pm0.019$ (binomial), consistent with an independent ray-projection prediction of $0.289$ and insensitive to the per-hit threshold over $0.5$--$5$~MeV ($0.2978\to0.2942$). The migration loss is geometric --- the A-arm counter at $+71.5^{\circ}$ places the median track impact ($x_c=-13.2$~cm) outside the $10$~cm counter half-width --- and no threshold choice in the scanned band recovers it; the hardware-selected sample is not a subset of the proxy sample ($54\%$ of hardware triggers lie outside the two-arm proxy definition). These are \evidence{MC\_TRIGGER\_MIGRATION} statements about the simulation's selection definitions (contract \texttt{TRIGGER\_HARDWARE\_RESPONSE}, evidence state \texttt{MIGRATION\_VALIDATED}); they do not bind real trigger electronics, whose response remains external (ADR-0002 items 1/3/4 in force), and production Sample-I/II membership continues to use the \evidence{MC\_TRIGGER\_PROXY} classifier.
