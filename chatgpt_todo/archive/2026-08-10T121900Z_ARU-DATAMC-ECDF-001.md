## 1. Atomic input/output contract

Input sample: pairs `(x_i, w_i)` where `x_i` is the comparison observable in its source units (DATA B2 ADC after reconstruction, or scaled MC energy in the same comparison coordinate) and `w_i` is a nonnegative dimensionless empirical-measure mass. The helper requires one-dimensional finite values, one-dimensional finite weights of equal length, nonnegative weights, at least one row, and positive total weight.

Output empirical distribution:

`F_w(x) = sum_i w_i I(x_i <= x) / sum_i w_i`.

The representation returned by `_weighted_ecdf` is `(support, cumulative_mass)` with one support row per unique value carrying the aggregate mass of every tied observation. `F_w` is right-continuous, nondecreasing, zero below the minimum positive-mass observation, and one at/above the maximum observation when total mass is positive.

The observed two-sample discrepancy is

`D = sup_x |F_data(x) - F_model(x)|`,

a dimensionless number in `[0,1]`. For two right-continuous finite empirical step functions, the difference is constant between successive values in the union of supports, so evaluating both CDFs at every union support point captures the supremum; the pre-first-support left limit is zero for both.

Provenance: implementation in `scripts/compare_data_mc.py`; adversarial tests in `tests/test_compare_data_mc_ecdf_contract.py`; existing integration tests updated in `tests/test_compare_data_mc.py`.

## 2. Competing mathematical mechanisms

- **H1 — right-continuous weighted ECDF:** aggregate tie mass, evaluate by `x_i <= x`. **SURVIVES / implemented.**
- **H2 — left-continuous empirical step:** uses strict `<`. Mathematically coherent diagnostic but not the declared CDF convention. **ELIMINATED for this statistic.**
- **H3 — piecewise-linear interpolation between observed rows:** old `np.interp` behavior. Produces non-empirical mass between observations. **ELIMINATED by deterministic counterexample.**
- **H4 — histogram/interpolated CDF:** potentially useful approximation, but bin-dependent and a different statistic. **ELIMINATED as KS/EDF implementation.**
- **H5 — row-wise cumulative values without explicit tie collapse:** can encode representation/order artifacts at repeated support. **COLLAPSED into H1 by aggregate empirical measure.**

The old two-point falsifier is exact: for values `[0,1]` with equal weights, an ECDF requires `F(0.5)=1/2`. Linear interpolation between cumulative rows `(0,1/2)` and `(1,1)` gives `3/4`, so H3 cannot be the declared ECDF.

## 3. Invariants, limits, identifiability

Required and tested:

1. `F_w(x < min X)=0`.
2. `F_w(x >= max X)=1` for positive total weight.
3. The function is constant between adjacent support values.
4. At a tied value `a`, jump size is `sum_{i:x_i=a} w_i / sum_i w_i`.
5. Permuting rows cannot change the empirical measure.
6. Replacing `(x,w)` by `k` identical rows `(x,w/k)` cannot change `F_w` or `D`, except floating roundoff.
7. Equal weights reduce exactly to the ordinary empirical CDF and ordinary two-sample KS `D`.
8. Quantization/saturation ties are statistical support multiplicities, not independent support values.

Dimensional check: weights cancel in the normalized ratio, so both `F` and `D` are dimensionless regardless of whether the comparison coordinate is ADC or scaled-MeV/ADC. This repair does not validate the physical scale transformation.

## 4. Eliminated combinations and surviving nuisances

Eliminated by source semantics and tests:

- interpolation-induced pseudo-mass between observations;
- treating duplicate tied rows as distinct CDF jump locations;
- treating row splitting as new empirical information;
- tie order as a scientific nuisance parameter.

Surviving dependencies are deliberately not averaged away:
