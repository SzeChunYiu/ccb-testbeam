# S39c: likelihood PID boundaries versus multitask pulse-representation classifiers

## Abstract

Ticket `1784176179.839.48902217` asks where traditional likelihood-ratio PID boundaries from charge, time-over-threshold proxies, and template-shape residuals disagree with multitask learned pulse representations. The study reuses the keyed digitized GEANT4 native-join waveform panel as a fixed upstream benchmark, directly rescans raw ROOT for the selected-pulse anchor in this ticket directory, and adds nuisance-residualized PID, boundary-disagreement maps, run/species bootstrap intervals, and stress-slice systematics. The winner written to `result.json` is **`template_residual_boosted_stack_new`**.

## Raw ROOT Reproduction

Raw B-stack files are read from `data/root/root`. This S39c runner scans `h101/HRDv` directly, reshapes the branch to `(event, channel, 18 samples)`, subtracts the four-sample pedestal `b_c=median(x_c[0:4])`, and selects B2/B4/B6/B8 pulses satisfying `max_t[x_c(t)-b_c]>1000 ADC`. Per-run counts are written to `reproduction_counts_by_run.csv`; the source G4 benchmark reproduction table is retained only as a cross-check.

## Data, Split, And Labels

The event-level benchmark has `1056` keyed digitized rows joined by native DAQ/G4 keys. Training runs are `[50, 51, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 57]` and held-out runs are `[58, 60, 62, 64, 65]`; the split is disjoint by run. PID labels are GEANT4 Sci-bar dominant proton/deuteron labels, energy is Sci-bar total deposited energy, timing is energy-weighted Sci-bar hit time, pile-up is the controlled-overlap truth flag, saturation is the digitized high-amplitude truth flag, and pedestal is the retained raw pedestal term.

## Methods

The traditional comparator is `deltaE_over_E_likelihood_template`, a dE/E-like likelihood/template pulse-shape boundary model using charge, shape residuals, and timing-proxy information. The ML/NN panel covers ridge/logistic baselines, gradient-boosted trees, MLP, 1D-CNN, the small `joint_sequence_transformer`, and the new `template_residual_boosted_stack_new` architecture. All model scores are inherited from the fixed upstream run-heldout panel so S39c changes the scientific endpoint, not the fitted models.

For each method `m`, the nuisance-only PID model is fitted on train runs as `q(z)=P(Y=1|z)` using `z=(E,t,pedestal,pileup,saturation,dE/dx,depth,shape area)`. The observed method score `s_m` is also regressed on `z`; the residualized score is `r_m=s_m-E[s_m|z]`. The main S39c PID endpoint is `AUC(Y,r_m)` on held-out runs. Boundary disagreement is `1[1{s_m>=0.5} != 1{s_trad>=0.5}]`, tabulated by run, PID species, energy, timing, pile-up, pedestal, saturation, and stave.

The winner minimizes

`0.36(1-AUC_resid)+0.16(1-purity)+0.16(1-efficiency)+0.10|rho(score,dt)|+0.08|median(dE/E)|+0.06 miss_pileup+0.05 max(0,AUC_unsat-AUC_sat)+0.03 pedestal_span`.

Confidence intervals are 95% percentile intervals from bootstrap resampling of held-out `(source_run, pid_name)` blocks.

## Systematics And Caveats

- The upstream benchmark is a keyed hybrid digitization: GEANT4 truth labels are joined to raw-template/residual waveforms, not a second independent detector run.
- Residualization removes information predictable from the listed nuisance coordinates under a train-fitted quadratic logistic/linear control model; unmeasured correlations can remain.
- The run/species bootstrap covers observed held-out source-run and PID-label variation, but not GEANT4 physics-list uncertainty or unseen beam tunes.
- Saturation robustness is measured by a digitized high-amplitude proxy; it should be read as an architecture stress test, not a hardware saturation calibration.
- Because the source model panel is fixed, this ticket evaluates PID-boundary behavior of already-trained methods rather than retuning architectures for the residualized endpoint.

## Verdict

`template_residual_boosted_stack_new` is the S39c winner. The residualization table shows whether the method keeps PID separation after timing, energy, pedestal, pile-up, saturation, dE/dx, depth, and gross shape controls; the disagreement and stress-strata tables show where likelihood PID boundaries diverge most from learned pulse representations.
