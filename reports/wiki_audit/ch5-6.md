## (b) Outliers + root cause

- **O1 — Why the two §6.2 sources disagree.** P02-discovery is an early exploratory PCA-vs-AE fit
  (in-sample, whole-dataset). P01 is the rigorous re-run with train/eval split **by run** (holdout runs
  42/57/64/65) and a masked *denoising* AE. Under the honest split the AE's dim-4 edge evaporates (PCA
  0.01337 < AE 0.01428). Root cause: the AE's low-dim advantage in P02 was partly in-sample overfit /
  favourable normalisation; it does not survive by-run generalisation. The wiki narrates the optimistic
  (P02) result while linking the pessimistic (P01) artifact.
- **O2 — Why ML wins two-pulse at full coverage but loses at matched 80% (§5.3).** At matched 80% both
  methods abstain on their least-confident 20%; the template fit, when it *does* answer, is essentially
  exact (failure 0.000, σ68 0.64 ns) because the fit kernel is the digitizer-card family — it either
  resolves the pair cleanly or abstains. ML answers everywhere with a softer score, so forcing full
  coverage it fails 4× less than the fit forced to full coverage (0.011 vs 0.048), but its resolution on
  the shared subset is coarser (σ68 0.89 ns). Verdict is genuinely operating-point dependent, not an
  artifact. Backed by `mc03_…/failure_at_coverage.csv` + `common_subset_sigma68.csv`.
- **O3 — Why the early-peak class is instrumental, not C12 (§6.3).** MV6b's decisive observable: with
  physical Birks quenching, C12 recoils (few-MeV, quench ~60–100×) sit at the noise floor — 0/1,656 reach
  A>1000 at *any* gain 60–297, and MC early-peak fraction is 0.000% vs data 4.4%. Since MC species content
  cannot produce A>1000 early-peak pulses at all, the class cannot be scintillation/species; it must be
  instrumental (baseline/bipolar/noise) or trigger-phase (MC pins peak phase at the 50 ns trigger, so MC
  structurally can't make them). S27 adds: excluding the class *raises* τ_eff (+13 ns), opposite the
  MC–data +8% offset, so it is not the offset's cause either.
- **O4 — Why τ_eff shifts +8% MC-vs-data (§5.2).** MC03 MC pulses are clean single pulses by construction
  (per-stave data-tuned exponential tails) → longer measured 10%-crossing live-time (134.99 ns), dominated
  by B2 at 141.35 ns. Data 124.79 ns is pulled *down* by real pathologies (early-peak/short-area events,
  the S27 class). The offset is an honest MC-idealisation gap, and — per line 399 — if real it tightens the
  R_max upper bound rather than validating 4.22.
