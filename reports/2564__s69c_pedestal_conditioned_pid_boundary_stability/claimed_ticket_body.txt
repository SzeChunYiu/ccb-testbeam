# NEW S69c pedestal-conditioned PID boundary stability with timing-energy pulse representations

Academic-grade study. Deepen pedestal, PID, energy, shape, and timing understanding by testing whether PID boundary drift is caused by pedestal state, pulse timing, pile-up, or saturation rather than particle response. Traditional methods: deltaE-E cuts, likelihood templates, calibrated logistic/isotonic PID scores, and pedestal-stratified sideband subtraction. ML/NN methods: ridge and gradient-boosted trees on charge, timing, pedestal, saturation, and shape atoms; MLP multitask PID-energy model; 1D-CNN waveform classifier/regressor; and transformer sequence model for event-window context where apt. Require run-family holdouts, shuffled-label and charge-only sentinels, calibration curves, fixed-purity efficiency, PID-energy coupling metrics, and bootstrap 95% CIs for all method deltas.
