## (b) Outliers + recursive root cause

**Central outlier — the 6.4-point gap (MC ideal trigger 99.7% B2 vs data Sample I 93.3%).**
Root cause is NOT geometry (material hypothesis falsified: ≤0.8 g/cm² available vs ≥10.5 needed) and
NOT the trigger *mechanism* (established: untriggered 45.9% → triggered 99.7% via a real `Trig_bar`
SD sim). It is the **idealization of the truth trigger** (noise-free, accidental-free, single-vertex).
NEW-04 (`new04_summary.json`) budgets it recursively — every term verified to trace:
- **Accidentals ~2.0 pt [1.3–2.6]** — first-principles `f_acc = R_B·Δt/(1+R_B·Δt)` with R_B≈R_max
  ≤3.05 MHz (one-sided UB) and Δt=15–30 ns, partner profile = Sample II (30.5% non-B2). Independently
  **data-anchored** by S10 (`…s10_pileup_rate_model`): current-driven downstream excess +1.03 pt
  [0.64, 1.42] (2.31%→3.34% from 2→20 nA) — same mechanism, same scale. Well-constrained.
- **Paddle fidelity ~1.5 pt [0.5–3.7]** — real deep-proton A-firing above the idealized 0.06% truth
  (p_deep 0.5–1.5% from δ-rays/straggling/real threshold). Largest single term but poorly bounded
  without a digitized-paddle model. Recursion stops here (needs new compute, not new physics insight).
- **Run-set/beam (B-M6) ±2 pt, sign unknown** — disjoint Sample-I/II run sets; owned by B-M6.
- **~2.9 pt genuinely unexplained** — no forced closure asserted. Honest residual.
All numbers reproduce from `new04_residual_budget.py`. **The recursion is sound and complete at the
artifact level; the only defect is that the WIKI does not surface it (see gap #3).**

**Second outlier — data S23 ratio 3.45 ≫ MC-truth S21 ratio 1.519.** These are **different
observables**, so their inequality is not a discrepancy:
- S21 **1.519** = ratio of deuteron *species fractions* (f_d,SI/f_d,SII = 0.675/0.444).
- S23 **3.45** = ratio of *high-amplitude tail fractions* f(A>5000)_SI/SII = 0.710/0.206.
A modest species-fraction enrichment (1.5×) is amplified into a large amplitude-tail contrast (3.45×)
because (i) deuterons deposit ~3× the energy (S21: 70.5 vs 24.6 MeV median), pushing them across the
A>5000 cut, and (ii) a hard amplitude threshold is nonlinear — it magnifies a fraction shift into a
tail-fraction shift, and (iii) the coincidence concentrates B2 occupancy. The gain-/geometry-robust
apples-to-apples comparison is the **double ratio (data I/II)/(MC I/II) = 0.738** (z −99): MC
*under*-predicts the contrast, exactly the missing-trigger signature. **Root cause reconciled;** the
wiki explains the double ratio but never explicitly tells the reader "3.45 and 1.519 measure different
things" — a reader will mis-read them as inconsistent (see viz #2).
